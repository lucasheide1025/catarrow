# Food and water economy audit

## Current chain

- Sources:
  - Shop bundles: 6 food or 6 water for 120 main-game coins (20 coins per unit).
  - Farm/water station: level 1 produces 2 units per week; level 20 produces 30 units per week.
- Storage: 36 units at warehouse level 0, rising to 156 at level 20.
- Sinks:
  - map movement: 0.25 food and water before VIT;
  - three-arrow combat: 1 food and water per round before VIT;
  - six-arrow combat: 2 food and water per round before VIT;
  - travel events can consume additional units.
- Unused carried supplies return to storage, so only actual consumption is an economic sink.
- Base commission coin rewards: T1 90, T2 170, T3 280, T4 440, T5 660, T6 980.

## Product decision

The low production rate is intentional:

- territory production is only a partial subsidy;
- regular play should continue spending main-game coins on supplies;
- expedition junk sales feed coins back into that loop;
- the system is also intended to address excess player coin balances.

Therefore the original production and shop values are retained.

## Retained values

- Shop bundle: 6 units for 120 coins (20 per unit).
- Production: level 1 produces 2 per week; level 20 produces 30 per week.
- Keep food and water symmetric until exploration events create meaningful asymmetry.
- Keep storage capacity unchanged.

Additional coin sinks should come from equipment enhancement and dismantling rather than increasing free supply production.
